River Becoming

2023 to 2024
Research Soundwalk Sound Performance

A year-long artistic research project approaching the Mur river in Graz through deep listening, public soundwalks, and sensory engagement with the river's ecological and social landscapes.

River Becoming is a research and performance project taking the Mur/Mura river as a site of ecological memory, multispecies agency, and communal listening. The project gathered photographers, sound artists, writers, and performers, each bringing their own sensory approach to the river and its non-human inhabitants.

In 2024, six soundwalks open to the public took place along the Mur in Graz, each facilitated by a different artist with their own methods of deep listening. Our intention was also to create space for collective celebrating and sharing of memories and personal connections to Mur. In that process, Mura became a collaborator, a witness and a medium.

The project resulted in the art book and exhibition Murky Voices (2025), gathering photographs, soundwalk scores, written works, and video installations as outcomes of the research.

The exhibition brought together the outputs of the River Becoming artistic research: video installations, printed photography, sound works, interviews, and the art book Murky Voices. The Mur river was framed as a site of ecological memory, multispecies agency, and sensory encounter.

River Becoming 2024 was supported by Stadt Graz and Land Steiermark.
