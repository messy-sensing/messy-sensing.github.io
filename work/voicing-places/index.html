Voicing Places

2024 to 2025
Sound Performance Participatory

An artistic research platform amplifying embodied, community-based reflections on listening and aural practice — working across sound art, sonic journalism, and soundwalking.

Voicing Places embraces the instability of sensing and situated bodies. Through phenomenological, sensory approaches, it explores how historical, social and political forces shape places and subjects.

Soundwalks, participatory events, public interventions, and mixed-media artworks with Anna Jurkiewicz (lecture performance), Lake Urumia (live electronics), Carmen Pomet, Fabien Artal, and Adina Camhy.

The project investigates embodied listening not as a passive act but as a form of situated knowledge production — one that honours the multiplicity of sensory experiences across different bodies and communities.
